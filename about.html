01 / THE STUDIO

We believe great photography is not simply about seeing what is in front of you.

It is about understanding the moment, anticipating the emotion and knowing exactly when to press the shutter.

XCY EVENTS is a photography and videography company based in Kampala, Uganda, creating visual stories across East Africa.

Our work is built around people, atmosphere, culture and the moments that make an occasion unforgettable.

CHRISTOPHER GALUKANDE FOUNDER / C.E.O
02 / THE FOUNDER

Led by
vision.

At the heart of XCY EVENTS is its founder, Mr Galukande Christopher.

Christopher's approach to visual storytelling has shaped XCY into a company focused on more than simply documenting events.

Every frame is approached with intention — from composition and lighting to timing, emotion and the atmosphere surrounding a moment.

Together with a team of skilled creatives, photographers and filmmakers, he continues to build visual experiences for clients across Uganda and East Africa.
